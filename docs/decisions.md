2026-10-07: P3 decisions confirmed by the user — (1) quantity is checked cumulatively: billed against received per PO line across all earlier non-duplicate invoices on that PO, not per invoice (the synthetic data has one invoice per PO, so results do not differ; it is the realistic rule); (2) a line that cannot be matched with confidence, and the defensive findings (PO belongs to a different supplier, line or total arithmetic off, billed over the ordered quantity, no receiving record, unknown supplier), end in `needs_review`, not `flag` — they are untested by the synthetic data and therefore not part of the gate; (3) the LLM line-match confidence threshold starts at 0.85 and is set on the dev set; (4) a price below the PO is not flagged, only overcharges (as logged 2026-10-03).

2026-10-09: Every form page has a time limit and a completion page is finished like a browser would — an opened page is a waiting n8n execution; measured: a decision page left alone ended as `success` after its limit with nothing recorded, a completion page ends the run by POSTing to itself, and a client that never does leaves the run waiting forever (`waitTill` in the year 3000). So list and decision pages wait at most 30 minutes, completion pages 10, the scripted client finishes completion pages, and the pipeline check fails if any run of the Approval Form is left waiting
2026-10-09: `request_approval` is called from the `Reconcile Invoice` sub-workflow, continue-on-fail, and its outcome rides in the reconciliation result — every path that reconciles (the upload, `POST /webhook/reconcile`, re-runs) therefore creates, refreshes or reports the approval with no second call to remember; if it fails, the invoice and its reconciliation stay stored, the response says `approval: pending` with the error, and reconciling again creates it
2026-10-09: A decided approval is never reopened, rewritten or deleted by the application — final decisions keep the audit trail honest; if a reconciliation re-run later disagrees with what the approver saw, the decision stands, the approval is reported `stale` and `approval.stale` is audited. A mistaken approval needs a database change, which the append-only audit log would show. Listed as a known limit, not a feature gap to quietly close
